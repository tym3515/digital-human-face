This project freely displays two predefined talking avatars. Its GLB import accepts the visitor's own compatible file and does not offer sample-based character creation. Attribution is shown voluntarily in the viewport.

Adaptations: female shorts removed and native leg topology adapted into opaque blue straight-leg jeans retaining its skin weights; modest adult body proportions baked consistently into body vertices, native morph targets and joint bind matrices; native humanoid bone names and five-vowel / eyelid / expression targets mapped to application controls; hair and garment material factors adjusted; native face UVs, skin weights, full body and mouth interior retained; embedded textures compressed to 1024px WebP and buffers packed with Meshopt. No facial portrait is projected onto these models and no extra lip mesh is overlaid. These are cartoon characters, not real people or face clones.

Female outerwear additionally extends the native cardigan into a sand knee-length coat, with pelvis-weighted lower panels, a narrow ivory crew-neck trim and added buttons; sleeves retain native arm weights.
